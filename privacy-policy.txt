Privacy Policy

App: QR Shop (com.qrshop.myanmar)
Developer: QR Menu Myanmar
Last updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Menu Myanmar (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the QR Shop mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

QR Shop is an e-commerce / ordering app that lets customers browse products, manage delivery addresses, place orders, view membership benefits and coupons, and receive related notifications. Customer and order data is processed through our backend systems and our business systems (including Odoo).

For privacy questions, contact us at: support@qrmyanmar.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

Account and profile information
- Login credentials (such as username/phone and password) used to sign in
- Customer profile details linked to your account (for example name, phone number, member code)
- Membership level, status, tickets, and coupon information
- Personalization tags used for features such as “Just for you”

Order and delivery information
- Delivery addresses and related contact details
- Order contents, quantities, prices, notes, and order history
- Payment-related information you submit in the App (for example payment method details you enter and payment proof screenshots you upload)

Device and app information
- Device identifiers and push notification tokens (to send order, product, or membership notifications)
- App usage / analytics events (via Firebase Analytics), such as screens viewed and basic app interaction data
- Local app preferences stored on your device (for example language, theme, login session token, cart contents)

Permissions the App may request
- Photos / media: to let you upload payment screenshots
- Notifications: to send push notifications about orders, products, or membership
- Microphone (Android declaration): may appear due to libraries used by the App; we do not use microphone audio for our core shopping features

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:
- Create and manage your account and login session
- Show products, prices (including membership pricelist prices where applicable), and personalized recommendations
- Process checkout, delivery, and order tracking
- Apply membership benefits and coupons
- Send transactional or service notifications (for example order updates, product alerts, coupon availability)
- Improve App performance, reliability, and user experience through analytics
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:
- Service providers / infrastructure partners that help us run the App, such as cloud hosting, databases/business systems (Odoo), push notification services (Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging), and analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)
- Delivery / operations staff as needed to fulfill your orders
- Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or business

These partners process data only as needed to provide their services to us and are expected to protect it appropriately.

5. Data retention

We retain account, order, address, membership, and related records for as long as needed to provide the App, fulfill orders, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Local data on your device (such as session tokens and cart data) remains until you log out, clear app data, or uninstall the App.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including authenticated API access and secure transmission to our servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

- You can update delivery addresses and account-related details in the App where available.
- You can disable push notifications in your device settings.
- You can log out to remove the local session token from the App.
- You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account/personal data by contacting us at the email above. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account except where we must retain information for legal, security, or legitimate business record-keeping purposes (for example completed order records).

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International processing

Our servers and service providers may process data in countries other than Myanmar (for example cloud hosting or analytics providers). By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
